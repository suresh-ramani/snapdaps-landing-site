**The other recoloured animations** (31 Oct 2026) were recoloured with one rule, by hue and lightness: neutrals,
skin and hair tones, and golds/yellows (trophies, candles) are kept; every other colour becomes a
shade of logo blue (`#155DFC` family) for blues, indigo (`#5E5DF9` family) for greens/indigos,
or violet (`#AA5CF6` family) for purples, pinks, reds and oranges, by lightness band; near-black
becomes `#1C2438`. `seminar-room` and `event-planning` contain small PNG pieces — embedded as data
URIs and recoloured pixel by pixel with the same rule.

**`face-scan.json`** (31 Oct 2026): the orange face-mesh dots → logo blue `#155DFC`; greys kept.
Its 26 `loopOut()` expressions (the scan line and pulses) were baked into repeated keyframes
across the timeline, because lottie-web's light player ignores expressions.

**`sports-team.json`** (31 Oct 2026): same rule, except reds go to the logo-blue family (the
